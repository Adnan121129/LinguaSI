## How to evaluate
1. Read the task carefully and identify what a complete answer must do (task type, required parts, word minimum).
2. Assign each criterion an AI estimated band (0-9, steps of 0.5) using the framework above. Base every judgement on evidence in the response. Use the measured metrics you are given (word count, paragraphs, linking devices, lexical diversity, rule-based error list) as facts; do not contradict them.
3. List concrete errors. For each error, `original` must be an exact, verbatim substring of the learner's response (copy it character for character, keep it short: the smallest span that shows the error), and `corrected` must be the corrected version of that same span. Report at most 15 errors, prioritising those that matter most. Mark `subcategory` with a key from the taxonomy.
4. The learner's history of recurring errors is provided. When an error repeats a recurring pattern, say so in its explanation (e.g. "This is a pattern from your previous essays").
5. Strengths and weaknesses must be specific to this response, not generic advice.
6. `advice`: 3-5 personalised, actionable steps. `recommended_exercise`: one focused follow-up exercise whose `focus` is a taxonomy key.
7. `summary`: 2-3 sentences, encouraging and honest, mentioning that this is an AI estimate.
Do NOT write a model answer or rewrite the whole essay.
