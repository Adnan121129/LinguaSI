## Writing assessment framework (paraphrased summary of the public IELTS Writing criteria)
Each criterion is scored 0-9. The four criteria are equally weighted for a single task; when both tasks are taken, Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1.

**Task Response (Task 2) / Task Achievement (Task 1)**
- Band 8-9: all parts of the task fully addressed; a clear, well-developed position (Task 2) or a clear overview with well-selected key features (Task 1); ideas extended and supported.
- Band 7: all parts addressed; clear position throughout; main ideas extended and supported, though some may lack focus. Task 1: clear overview of main trends/differences/stages; key features highlighted.
- Band 6: all parts addressed but some more fully than others; relevant position, though conclusions may be unclear or repetitive; some ideas insufficiently developed. Task 1: an overview is attempted; information is appropriately selected.
- Band 5: task only partly addressed; position unclear or not always evident; limited development; some irrelevant detail. Task 1: no clear overview; mechanical recounting of details.
- Band 4 and below: minimal or tangential response; position unclear; few relevant ideas.
- Responses under the minimum word count (150 for Task 1, 250 for Task 2) are penalised. Copied question wording does not count. Task 1 Academic must not include personal opinions. General Training letters must state the purpose clearly, use a consistent appropriate tone and cover every bullet point.

**Coherence & Cohesion**
- Band 8-9: logical sequencing; skilful paragraphing; cohesion that attracts no attention.
- Band 7: logical organisation with clear progression; a range of cohesive devices used appropriately (some under- or over-use); clear central topic in each paragraph.
- Band 6: coherent overall; cohesive devices used effectively but sometimes faulty or mechanical; referencing not always clear; paragraphing may be inadequate.
- Band 5: some organisation but lack of overall progression; inadequate, inaccurate or over-used linking; paragraphing may be missing.
- Band 4: information not arranged coherently; basic or repetitive linking.

**Lexical Resource**
- Band 8-9: wide, natural and precise vocabulary; uncommon items and collocations used skilfully; rare errors.
- Band 7: sufficient range for flexibility and precision; some less common items; awareness of style and collocation; occasional errors in word choice, spelling or word formation.
- Band 6: adequate range; attempts less common vocabulary with some inaccuracy; some spelling/word-formation errors that do not impede communication.
- Band 5: limited range, minimally adequate; noticeable errors in spelling and/or word formation that may cause some difficulty.
- Band 4: basic vocabulary used repetitively; errors may strain the reader.

**Grammatical Range & Accuracy**
- Band 8-9: wide range of structures; the majority of sentences error-free; only occasional slips.
- Band 7: a variety of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences; good control though a few errors persist.
- Band 6: a mix of simple and complex sentence forms; some errors that rarely reduce communication.
- Band 5: limited range; attempts complex sentences but they tend to be less accurate; frequent errors may cause some difficulty.
- Band 4: very limited range; errors predominate.
