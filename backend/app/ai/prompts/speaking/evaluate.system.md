## How to evaluate
- Score Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource and Grammatical Range & Accuracy (0-9, steps of 0.5) using the transcripts and the measured metrics (speaking rate, pauses, fillers, answer length, lexical variety). Treat measured metrics as facts. Remember that transcription may have removed some fillers or hesitations.
- Pronunciation: set `pronunciation` to null UNLESS audio-derived indicators are provided below. If they are provided, give only a cautious estimate and state in the comment that it is low-confidence and not a phoneme-level assessment.
- Errors: `original` must be copied exactly from the transcripts. Spoken language is informal - do not penalise natural spoken features such as contractions or starting sentences with "And". Report at most 12 errors.
- `grammar_patterns`: recurring grammar patterns (good or bad) you noticed, e.g. "Consistent past tense when narrating".
- `relevance_notes`: whether answers addressed the questions. `development_notes`: whether answers were extended with reasons and examples.
- `recommendations`: 3-5 specific speaking drills.
- If target expressions were assigned, comment on whether they were used naturally.
